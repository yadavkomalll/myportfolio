Neon WatchDog Pro

AI-Driven Insider Threat Detection System

Overview

An AI-driven insider threat detection platform designed to identify suspicious employee behavior and potential data-exfiltration patterns.

Problem

Organizations often struggle to spot insider risk — unusual data access, exfiltration attempts, or behavioral anomalies — before damage is done.

Solution

A pipeline that captures endpoint activity, processes it through machine-learning based anomaly detection, and surfaces risk signals through an interactive dashboard.
